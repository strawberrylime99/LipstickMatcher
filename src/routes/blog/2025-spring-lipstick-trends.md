Spring is finally here, and that means it's time to refresh your makeup routine with the latest lip colors. 💋✨

## 1. Soft Petal Pinks 🌸

From ballet slipper to rose quartz, petal pinks are back — and they’re softer than ever. These shades pair perfectly with dewy skin and natural lashes.

## 2. Bold Peach & Coral 🍑

Corals are back in a big way for Spring 2025. Pair your sun-kissed cheeks with a punchy peach or juicy tangerine lip. Bonus points if you layer it with a glossy topcoat.

## 3. AI-Assisted Matching 🧠💄

Finding your perfect match just got easier. With tools that analyze your skin tone and undertone, you can discover lipstick shades that truly complement your complexion — no guesswork required.

---

**Final thoughts:** Whether you’re into bold hues or subtle sheers, there’s something for every vibe this spring. Try exploring AI-powered tools for personalized picks that go beyond the beauty counter.
